Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026
App: Smile Alarm (com.megupen.smilealarm)
Provider: Megupen

1. Camera and face analysis

The smile-dismiss feature uses the front camera. Camera frames and face analysis are processed in real time on the device.

  • The app does not save camera images, video, face images, or facial landmarks.
  • The app does not send camera images, video, face images, or facial landmarks to the provider or a third-party server.
  • During analysis, the app temporarily uses only a numerical smile score.

If camera access is denied, the standard stop or the 30-second emergency hold remains available.

2. Data stored on the device

The app stores the following data on the device:

  • Alarm time, repeat days, sound, enabled state, and related settings
  • Smile scores, measurement times, and dismissal method
  • App preferences and onboarding status
  • The free-trial start time
  • The purchase status last verified through Google Play

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled. Stored data remains on the device until the user deletes it or uninstalls the app.

3. Purchases through Google Play

Google Play Billing is used for the one-time purchase and purchase verification. Google handles payment details such as card numbers; the app provider does not receive them. The purchase screen uses the price returned by Google Play.

4. Off-device transfer and third-party sharing

The app does not send camera frames, face data, smile scores, alarm settings, or dismissal history to the provider's servers or to advertising companies. It does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Permissions and special access

  • Camera: smile detection
  • Notifications: ringing alerts and access to the dismissal screen
  • Exact alarms: ringing at a user-selected time
  • Full-screen intents: showing the dismissal screen over the lock screen
  • Device boot events: restoring alarms after a restart
  • Read access to an audio file selected by the user: playing that file as an alarm sound

6. Review and deletion

Users can stop recording smile scores or delete all saved records from Settings. Record deletion remains available after the free trial ends. Individual alarms can be deleted from the alarm list. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data.

The app has no account-creation feature.

7. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children. It is not designed for users under 13, and it is not submitted under the Google Play Families Policy.

8. Security and changes

The app requests only the access needed for its features and is designed to keep camera frames and face data on the device. If features, SDKs, or data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated.
